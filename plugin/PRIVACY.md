# Privacy policy: stay-finder

stay-finder is an instructions-only plugin. It has no server, account, analytics or telemetry of its
own, and its author receives no data from anyone who uses it.

## What leaves your computer when the skill runs

The skill tells Claude to make these requests from your own machine:

| Sent to | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Airbnb (`www.airbnb.com`) | Search parameters: map area, check-in and check-out dates, number of adults, currency, budget, filters | To find listings and read their public pages |
| OpenStreetMap Nominatim (`nominatim.openstreetmap.org`) | The neighbourhood or city names you asked about | To get each area's outline for the map |
| Airbnb's image server (`a0.muscache.com`) | Requests for listing photos | To show and, if you ask, judge the photos |
| Esri (`server.arcgisonline.com`) | Map tile requests for the area shown | To draw the map on the results page |

No names, email addresses, account details, payment details or contact details are sent, read or
stored. Nothing is sent to the plugin's author.

## What is stored

The results page and any downloaded pages or photos are saved as files on your own computer, where you
asked for them. Favourites and deletions you mark on the page are kept in your browser's local
storage for that page. You can delete these files and that storage at any time.

Each service above handles its requests under its own privacy policy.
